Speech Split

Privacy and terms

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Speech Split does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your speeches, practice history and settings stay on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Speech Split is made by Dylan Scott Meschner, a private person in Norway. Contact: dylan123@live.no.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Speech Split is saved only on the device you use it on:

  • Android: in the app’s own storage. It may be included in your phone’s own Android backup, if you have that switched on.
  • Windows: in your user folder (%APPDATA%\Speech Split).
  • Web version: in your browser’s storage for this site. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Nothing of this is sent to me or anyone else. When you share or export a speech or a run report, you choose where it goes.

Documents you import

When you import a PDF, Word or text file, it is read on your device to suggest segments. The document is never uploaded.

Contact with the internet

  • Update check and patch notes: the Android and Windows apps ask GitHub whether a newer version exists, and the app and website load the patch notes from GitHub. Only public information about Speech Split is fetched; nothing about you or your speeches is sent. Like any website, GitHub can see your IP address when this happens (see GitHub’s privacy statement). In the apps you can turn the automatic check off under Settings → About.
  • This website and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security reasons. Speech Split itself uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Permissions on Android

  • Vibration: for the optional buzz at the warning and when time is up.
  • Notifications: only if you switch on “Buzz my smartwatch”. The notifications are silent and remove themselves.
  • Do Not Disturb access: only if you switch on “Do Not Disturb while speaking”. It is turned on while the timer runs and switched back off afterwards.
  • Internet: only for the update check and patch notes described above.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback, I receive what you write and your email address. I only use it to answer you and to improve Speech Split, and I delete it if you ask me to.

Your rights

Because Speech Split doesn’t collect personal data, there is nothing stored about you that I could show, correct or delete, apart from emails you have sent me. You can delete everything in the app at any time by deleting your speeches, or by uninstalling the app or clearing the site’s data in your browser. If you have questions, write to me. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Terms of use

  • Speech Split is free. You may use it privately, at work, in school or in your organisation.
  • It is provided “as is”, without any warranty. I work to keep it reliable, but I can’t guarantee that it is free of errors, or be held responsible for any loss that comes from using it, for example if a timer is wrong or data is lost. Keep your own copy of anything important.
  • Only download Speech Split from this website or from its GitHub page.
  • These pages may be updated when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
